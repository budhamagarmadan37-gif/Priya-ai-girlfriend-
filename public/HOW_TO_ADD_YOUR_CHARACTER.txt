========================================================================
             HOW TO ADD YOUR CUSTOM ANIME CHARACTER VIDEOS TO PRIYA
========================================================================

Since you have generated or uploaded 3 awesome videos of your custom character,
you can easily add them to the Priya AI Companion app!

Follow these simple steps to make your character come to life in the live preview:

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: Locate the 3 Video Files
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Find the three video files you generated/uploaded:
1. One for the Idle/Sleeping state (Priya waiting, blinking or breathing).
2. One for the Thinking state (Priya looking thoughtful or nodding).
3. One for the Speaking state (Priya talking and gesturing).

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: Rename the Files
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rename your 3 video files EXACTLY as follows (all lowercase, no spaces):
- Rename the idle video to:       priya_idle.mp4
- Rename the thinking video to:   priya_thinking.mp4
- Rename the talking video to:    priya_talking.mp4

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: Upload the Files to the "public" Folder
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. In the file explorer on the left sidebar of Google AI Studio, look for 
   the folder named "public".
2. Click on the "public" folder.
3. Click the Upload button (or drag and drop your three renamed files) 
   directly into the "public" folder.
4. Verify that the files are now placed inside the public directory:
   - public/priya_idle.mp4
   - public/priya_thinking.mp4
   - public/priya_talking.mp4

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4: Turn On and Enjoy!
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Once the files are uploaded, refresh the application preview or click the 
Power button to wake up Priya. She will load your custom videos and start 
talking, thinking, and interacting with you in real-time with her lively, 
intelligent, and flirty personality!
